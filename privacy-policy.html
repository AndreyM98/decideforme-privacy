Privacy Policy

Decide For Me  ·  Last updated: April 28, 2026

Short version: Decide For Me does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email, location, or any other identifier.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores your saved option sets locally on your device using Android's SharedPreferences (a standard on-device storage mechanism). This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not transmitted to any server
  • Is deleted when you uninstall the app

3. Analytics & Tracking

The app contains no analytics, no tracking SDKs, no crash reporting, and no advertising of any kind.

4. Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate any third-party services, APIs, or SDKs beyond the standard Flutter framework and Android platform libraries.

5. Permissions

The app requests no special Android permissions (no camera, microphone, contacts, location, internet, or storage access).

6. Children's Privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. Since we collect no data whatsoever, there is no risk to children's privacy.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
